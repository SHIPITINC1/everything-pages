Privacy policy.

How EVERYTHING handles information in the initial iOS version.

Effective: November 14, 2026 · Applies to the initial iOS app version (0.2.0)

Pre-release draft. This document describes the current source-code implementation. The privacy disclosures must be rechecked against the final shipped app before App Store submission.

1. What EVERYTHING does

EVERYTHING provides a combined feed of selected publicly accessible content from Bluesky, Mastodon and YouTube, as available, plus shortcuts to independently operated social-network websites. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by those platforms.

2. Information stored on your device

The current version stores your bookmarks, selected public-feed handles or hashtags, YouTube channel IDs and a draft message in the device's local app preferences storage. You can view or change these through the app. The developer does not operate a user account service or backend that receives this information in the initial version.

Bookmarks may contain copies of public-post text, author names, source links and timestamps. This information remains in the app's local storage in the current version.

3. Public feeds and third-party requests

To fetch public content, the app contacts Bluesky, Mastodon and YouTube using HTTPS. Depending on your feed settings, requests can disclose the public handles, hashtags, or channel IDs you ask to load, plus typical network information such as your IP address and request time. These services process information according to their own privacy policies.

Links that open other social platforms use Apple's Safari browser interface. Those websites may use login sessions, cookies, trackers or other data practices governed by their own policies. EVERYTHING does not control those websites.

4. Accounts, passwords and analytics

The initial app does not request or store passwords to your social-network accounts; does not create an EVERYTHING user account; and does not include an app analytics, advertising or tracking SDK in its current source code. It does not sell personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Bookmarks, feed preferences and drafts remain in local app storage until changed, deleted, or the application is removed from the device. Deleting EVERYTHING removes its local app data through the operating system, subject to any device backups maintained by Apple. To manage data held by third-party networks, use those networks' privacy controls.

6. Children's privacy

EVERYTHING is not designed specifically for children. External websites have their own age requirements and content moderation practices. Parents and guardians should consider the rules of each linked social service.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date shown above will be revised when applicable.

8. Contact
