Frequently asked questions

Why are some social networks only shortcuts?

Some platforms restrict third-party access to personal home feeds. Their tiles open official websites rather than copying private feeds.

Which social networks appear in the combined feed?

The first version aims to show selected publicly available posts from Bluesky, public Mastodon hashtags, and YouTube channel uploads. Sources must be configured and available; a provider can deny or limit requests.

Do I need to share my social-media passwords?

No. EVERYTHING's public feed reader does not ask for social-media passwords. When you open a social website, authentication occurs directly on that website.

Why isn't a feed loading?

Check your connection, verify the Bluesky handle or Mastodon hashtag, or enter a valid YouTube channel ID in the Sources tab. A service may be temporarily unavailable or prohibit anonymous public requests.

How do I remove my bookmarks and drafts?

Delete individual bookmarks in Saved. Drafts can be cleared in Studio. Deleting the app clears its local data, subject to normal iOS device backups.

Can I publish one post everywhere?

No. The first version includes a local draft editor; it does not automatically publish to social networks.
